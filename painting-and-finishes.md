# Painting and finishes

Room by room, so every quantity can be found on the plan. Worked example: [painting takeoff](https://github.com/Grishutov/takeoff-samples#3-painting-takeoff---residence-bedroom-wing).

## Read first
- [ ] Finish schedule: floor, skirting, walls and ceiling for every room.
- [ ] Paint systems by surface: walls, ceilings, wet areas, doors and frames, metalwork.
- [ ] Ceiling heights from sections or the reflected ceiling plan. If there are none, assume, flag the cells and add a query.
- [ ] Tiled areas and feature walls: these are not painted, or painted differently.

## Walls
- [ ] Perimeter x height per room.
- [ ] Deduct openings by your stated rule (for example, openings over 0.5 m2).
- [ ] Add reveals at windows and doors where they are painted.
- [ ] Different systems or colours as separate lines.

## Ceilings
- [ ] Area = floor area for flat ceilings. True area for sloped ceilings.
- [ ] Bulkheads and soffits separately.

## Doors, frames and trims
- [ ] Doors counted by type, both faces, plus edges if specified.
- [ ] Frames and architraves in linear metres or by count, as the client prices them.
- [ ] Skirting = room perimeter minus door openings.

## Floors
- [ ] Area by finish type. Tile waste depends on the pattern: state it.
- [ ] Thresholds and transitions counted where two finishes meet.

## Check
- [ ] Sum of room floor areas against the gross internal area.
- [ ] Every room in the finish schedule appears in the takeoff, and the other way round.

## Deliver
- [ ] Excel room by room with drawing references, a summary by system, and the assumptions sheet.
- [ ] Colour PDF markup.
