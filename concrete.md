# Concrete

Net volumes by element, with formwork and reinforcement kept on their own lines.

## Read first
- [ ] Structural general notes: concrete grades, covers, exposure classes.
- [ ] Slab and wall thicknesses on every sheet. On some drawings sizes are in centimetres (d=50 means 500 mm). Search the whole set before writing "not shown".
- [ ] Foundation plan, sections and details: footings, ground beams, pile caps, thickenings, drop panels.
- [ ] Rebar schedules, if issued.

## Measure
- [ ] Split by element: footings, ground beams, slab on grade, suspended slabs, columns, walls, beams, stairs, external works.
- [ ] Avoid double counting at intersections. One common convention: slab through, beams measured below the slab soffit, columns between slabs. State the one you use.
- [ ] Deduct openings by the measuring rules in the contract and say which rules.
- [ ] Formwork by element: sides and soffits in m2, edges in linear metres.
- [ ] Blinding, membrane, insulation and sub-base under slabs as separate lines.
- [ ] Reinforcement from the schedules. If there are none, use a stated kg/m3 allowance per element and mark it as an allowance.

## Check
- [ ] A quick 3D model of the frame shows double counts and missed elements at once.
- [ ] Compare ratios with similar buildings: m3 of concrete per m2 of floor, kg of steel per m3.
- [ ] Every element on the foundation plan appears in the takeoff.

## Deliver
- [ ] Excel by element and level, with drawing references and dimensions.
- [ ] Markup or sketches for unusual elements.
- [ ] Assumptions: covers, conventions at intersections, allowances.
