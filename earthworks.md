# Earthworks

Survey and grading design to cut and fill volumes. The full method is in my guide [Cut and fill volumes](https://github.com/Grishutov/cad-guides/blob/main/cut-and-fill.md).

## Read first
- [ ] Survey: points, contours or a surface, the coordinate system and the height datum.
- [ ] Grading design: finished levels, falls, batters and the finished floor level.
- [ ] Pavement build-ups and slab build-ups: subgrade is below finished grade.
- [ ] Geotechnical report: topsoil depth, unsuitable material, rock, shrink and swell factors.

## Measure
- [ ] Build the existing surface with breaklines (top and toe of banks, ditches, kerbs) and a clear boundary.
- [ ] Build the design surface to subgrade, not to finished grade.
- [ ] Topsoil strip as its own line, not inside cut.
- [ ] Cut, fill and net by area or by phase if the client needs it.

## Check
- [ ] Compute the volume by a second method: grid or cross-sections against the surface-to-surface result.
- [ ] Explain any difference over a few percent.
- [ ] Draw the zero line and check that it makes sense on the plan.

## Deliver
- [ ] Cut, fill, net and topsoil volumes, with the shrink or swell factor stated and the import or export result.
- [ ] Cut and fill map, sections, and the list of assumptions.
