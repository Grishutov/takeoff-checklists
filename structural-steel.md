# Structural steel

A member list that a fabricator can price. Worked example and tools: [steel-takeoff-tools](https://github.com/Grishutov/steel-takeoff-tools).

## Read first
- [ ] Member schedule and marks, section sizes, steel grade.
- [ ] Plans and elevations together. A member shown on both is still one member.
- [ ] What is included: main members only, or also plates, bolts, connections, purlins and girts, stairs and handrails. Ask.

## Measure
- [ ] Count each mark on plan and elevation and note where it was counted.
- [ ] Length from grid dimensions and levels, by mark.
- [ ] Weight = length x mass per metre (or lb/ft) from the section table you state.
- [ ] Secondary steel (purlins, girts, bracing) on its own lines.
- [ ] Connections as a stated percentage allowance if they are not detailed.
- [ ] Coating area (m2 per tonne or per member) if painting or galvanising is in scope.

## Check
- [ ] Counts by mark against the schedule.
- [ ] Total weight against a kg/m2 ratio for a similar frame.
- [ ] Every gridline intersection has its columns.

## Deliver
- [ ] Bill of materials by mark: quantity, length, mass per metre, weight, subtotals by section.
- [ ] Colour markup with count tags and a legend.
- [ ] Allowance percentage, exclusions and queries.
