# Checking a BoQ

For checking a contractor's or another surveyor's quantities against the drawings. As head of a survey department I did this every month for contractors' payment claims.

## Before checking
- [ ] Confirm the drawing revision the quantities were based on.
- [ ] Confirm the measuring rules and units of the BoQ.
- [ ] Sort the items by value. Usually a small share of the items carries most of the cost: check those first.

## Check
- [ ] Re-measure the chosen items yourself before looking at their quantity, so their number does not guide you.
- [ ] Compare by element and location, not only the total. Totals can hide errors that cancel out.
- [ ] Explain every difference over 2 to 3 percent.

## Common causes of differences
- [ ] Double counting where elements meet: slab and beam, wall and column, plan and elevation.
- [ ] Gross quantities where net was required, or the other way round.
- [ ] Waste added to the quantity and again in the rate.
- [ ] Wrong units: m2 and m3, LF and SF, kg and tonnes.
- [ ] An old revision or a missing addendum.
- [ ] Items measured on a sheet that is not to scale.

## Deliver
- [ ] A table with item, their quantity, my quantity, the difference, the reason and the drawing reference.
- [ ] A short summary: what is right, what is over-measured, what is missing.
